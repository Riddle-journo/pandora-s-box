Accountability Scorecards

How documented governance failures were answered — rolled up by party, government term, ministry and sector. The accountability rate is the share of failures on that watch that saw real consequences (resignation, prosecution, remedy = full; inquiry-only = partial; nothing = none). Every party and government is scored by the identical formula on the same balanced corpus — judge the conduct, not the colours.

Follow the Money

Who funded which party, and from which industry — from the Electoral Bonds data the State Bank of India disclosed on Supreme Court / ECI order (Apr 2019 – Jan 2024). India discloses political funding at the party level, so each representative inherits their party's funding profile. Every party's disclosed funding is shown — figures are the public record, not a framing.
